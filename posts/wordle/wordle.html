The optimal Wordle play gives the distribution of possible mystery words with lowest entropy on average. A Wordle play gives you two pieces of information: the intersection and set difference of its letter set with that of the mystery word. For each possible information returned, we can count the number of 5-letter words consistent with this information. Taking the log gives the entropy of this remaining-word distribution. Average over possible information returned to get expected entropy. The whole computation is only a couple lines of Julia:

words = lowercase.(readlines(expanduser("~/Downloads/filtered-wordle-words.csv")))
df = @chain DataFrame(w=words, b=BitSet.(collect.(Int, words))) begin
    crossjoin(_, _, makeunique=true)
    @rtransform(:yes = intersect(:b, :b_1), :no=setdiff(:b, :b_1))
    groupby([:w, :yes, :no])
    @combine :n = length(:w_1)
    groupby(:w)
    @combine :entropy = sum((:n ./ sum(:n)) .* log.(:n))
    @orderby :entropy
end
